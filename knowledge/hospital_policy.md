# MediSlot Hospital Appointment and Booking Policy

This document outlines the scheduling policies, insurance mappings, cancellation rules, and prioritization framework for all departments within the MediSlot Hospital Network.

---

## 1. Booking Rules per Specialization

* **Cardiology**: 
  - Appointments must be booked at least 24 hours in advance. No same-day appointments are allowed due to the prep work required for diagnostic EKG scans.
  - New patients must have a baseline health questionnaire filled prior to confirmation.
* **Dermatology**:
  - Appointments can be booked up to 1 hour in advance.
  - Skin cancer screenings and biopsies require a minimum 45-minute slot.
* **General Medicine**:
  - Open for same-day bookings.
  - Serve as the primary entry point for referrals.
* **Orthopedics**:
  - Requires a referral from a General Practitioner (GP) for all new patient consultations.
  - Return visits do not require a new referral if within 6 months.
* **Pediatrics**:
  - Only open to patients under the age of 18.
  - A parent or legal guardian must accompany the patient.
* **ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat)**:
  - Routine cleanings and hearings tests do not require referral.
  - Complex surgical consults require a prior diagnostic audiology report.

---

## 2. Insurance Coverage Rules

Insurance plans accepted and covered by specific departments:
* **MediCare**:
  - 100% coverage for **General Medicine**, **Cardiology**, and **ENT**.
  - No coverage for elective Dermatology procedures.
* **ShieldCross**:
  - 100% coverage for **General Medicine**, **Dermatology**, and **Orthopedics**.
  - Cardiology requires a 20% co-pay.
* **CareFirst**:
  - 100% coverage for **Pediatrics** and **General Medicine**.
  - ENT and Dermatology require prior authorization.
* **No Insurance (Self-Pay)**:
  - Accepted in all departments.
  - Requires a flat $50 deposit at checkout before appointment confirmation.

---

## 3. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

* **Notice Period**: Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 12 hours prior to the scheduled start time.
* **Late Cancellation Fee**: Cancellations made less than 12 hours in advance will incur a $25 fee.
* **No-Show Rule**: Patients who miss 3 appointments within a 12-month period without notifying the hospital will be temporarily restricted from online booking and placed on phone-only scheduling.

---

## 4. Walk-In vs Appointment-Only Departments

* **Appointment-Only**: 
  - **Cardiology**, **Orthopedics**, and **Dermatology** are strictly appointment-only. Walk-in patients will be redirected to General Medicine for triage.
* **Walk-in Friendly**:
  - **General Medicine** and **Pediatrics** maintain dedicated walk-in hours between 08:00 and 11:00 daily on a first-come, first-served basis. Urgent walk-ins outside these hours are subject to doctor availability.

---

## 5. Priority Rules for Urgent Requests

* **Urgent Triage**: Patients experiencing acute symptoms (e.g., chest pain, shortness of breath, high fever in infants) must be prioritized.
* **Slot Bumping**: For documented urgent cardiology requests, the scheduling coordinator may bump routine consultations to clear a slot, provided the bumped patient is notified at least 4 hours in advance.
* **Emergency Department Redirect**: Life-threatening emergencies (e.g., suspected stroke, active heart attack, severe trauma) must NOT be booked via MediSlot AI and should be immediately redirected to the nearest Emergency Room.
